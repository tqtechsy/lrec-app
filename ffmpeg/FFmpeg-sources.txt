FFmpeg in LREC Converter
========================

ffmpeg.exe (in the "ffmpeg" folder next to the app) is FFmpeg 9.0.2, built with only the parts the app uses and
linked with x264 and LAME. It is a separate program the app starts; it is licensed under the GNU General Public
License version 3 (see FFmpeg-GPLv3.txt and FFmpeg-LICENSE.txt in this folder).

Sources of what is in it:
- FFmpeg 9.0.2: https://ffmpeg.org/releases/ffmpeg-9.0.2.tar.xz
- x264 0.165.r3222 (b35605a): https://code.videolan.org/videolan/x264
- LAME 3.100: https://lame.sourceforge.io
How it was built (configure options): build-ffmpeg.sh, published with the app's releases at
https://github.com/tqtechsy/lrec-app

The TrueSpeech decoder (truespeech.dll) is a port of FFmpeg's TrueSpeech decoder, licensed under the GNU Lesser
General Public License 2.1 or later (TrueSpeech-decoder-LGPL-2.1.txt). Its source is published at
https://github.com/tqtechsy/lrec-app. You may replace truespeech.dll with your own build of that source.
